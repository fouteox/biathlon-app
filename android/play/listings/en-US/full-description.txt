Follow the Biathlon World Cup season: every race, its start list and its results, live while the race is on.

• Home: the live race, otherwise the next one, and the latest podium.
• Calendar: every event and race of the season, and of past seasons.
• Results, live results and the race analysis: course, range and shooting times.
• World Cup standings, with your favorite athletes and nations highlighted.
• Athletes and nations: their results history and Wikipedia article.
• Favorites, kept on your device.
• Alerts when a race is cancelled or its start moves, and a reminder before the start.
• Live updates: follow a race from the lock screen and the status bar.
• A widget and shortcuts for the next race and the latest result.
• The TV channels showing each race, the race in your calendar, and the podium to share.

In English, French and German.

This app is not affiliated with the International Biathlon Union (IBU).
